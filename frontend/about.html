About EthioMap

Geographic information, made easier to explore.

EthioMap is a web-based Map Information Subsystem designed to help users view, explore, search, and understand geographic information about Ethiopia in one accessible place.

Our purpose

Support clearer location-based decisions.

The system brings map layers and spatial datasets together in an interactive map workspace. It makes geographic information easier to discover and use for learning, planning, research, and everyday exploration.

01

Interactive map

Explore Ethiopia using map controls, zoom tools, different basemaps, and location details.

02

Place search

Search for places, regions, coordinates, and available spatial datasets from the map workspace.

03

Spatial data layers

View saved GeoJSON datasets directly on the map and use them as part of your geographic exploration.

04

Map measurement

Measure distance and area on the map to better understand scale and spatial relationships.

Who it serves

Built for people working with Ethiopia’s geographic information.
